# Personal Brand AI — Page Specifications

## Public website

Landing communicates the professional operating system, AI-first content workflow, grounded output, and primary call to action. Pricing uses the centralized five-plan configuration. Sign in, sign up, and onboarding share the same typography, palette, controls, and trust-oriented visual language.

## Dashboard

Prioritize profile completeness, AI content generation, content pipeline, upcoming scheduled posts, recent posts, analytics/performance, AI recommendations, LinkedIn status, usage, and subscription. The page should feel like a polished command center rather than a metrics demo.

## Professional Profile and Resume

Use calm, editable forms grouped by user-owned facts. Show completeness and validation clearly. Resume upload/build and optimization must distinguish user facts from AI suggestions and keep all generated material reviewable.

## Content Studio and Post Review

Content Studio is an AI writing workspace, not a CRUD form. It includes topic/context, content pillar, audience, tone/style, additional context, generation progress, editable post body, character count, critique, improvement, save, approval, schedule, and publish actions. Post Review includes author/profile preview, quality indicators, AI critique, suggested improvements, and explicit approval.

## Content Ideas and Calendar

Ideas are lightweight, scannable inputs that can become drafts. Calendar supports month/week/day navigation, post previews, draft/scheduled/published statuses, and create-post action. The same post representation should carry through studio, calendar, lists, and history.

## All Posts, Analytics, LinkedIn, Billing, Settings

All Posts uses consistent status chips and post previews. Analytics uses believable, restrained charts for impressions, engagement, reactions, comments, shares, follower growth, posting frequency, top content, and AI insights; avoid giant fake-looking numbers. LinkedIn clearly distinguishes disconnected, connected, publishing, published, and failed states. Billing reflects centralized plan/usage configuration. Settings uses the same form and navigation system.

## Page quality checklist

Every page must have a purposeful header, a clear primary action, consistent spacing and controls, responsive behavior, polished empty/loading/error states, and no placeholder-looking or invented product behavior.
